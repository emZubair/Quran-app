# Privacy Policy — Offline Al Quran (Ads Free)

**Effective date:** July 11, 2026

## Summary

This app collects **no data**. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no user accounts, and no network requests. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

## What the app stores

The app saves the following **only on your device**, so it can restore your reading experience:

- Your bookmarks
- Your last-read position
- Your display settings (font size, font choice, translation visibility, dark mode)

This information never leaves your device. It is not transmitted, shared, or backed up to any server by the app. Deleting the app deletes this data.

## What the app does NOT do

- No personal information is collected or requested
- No analytics or crash-reporting services are used
- No advertising networks or SDKs are included
- No internet connection is used — the complete Quran text and translation are bundled inside the app
- No permissions are requested (no location, camera, microphone, contacts, etc.)

## Children

The app is safe for all ages. Since no data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children.

## Third-party content

The Quran text originates from the Tanzil project (tanzil.net), and the English translation is by Marmaduke Pickthall (1930, public domain). These are static texts bundled with the app; no third party receives any information about your use of the app.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes any of the above (for example, adding optional audio downloads), this policy will be updated and the change noted in the app's release notes before it takes effect.
