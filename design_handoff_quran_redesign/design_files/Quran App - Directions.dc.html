Two directions
1a treats the app as a printed mushaf — warm paper, serif, reading before everything. 1b treats it as a daily practice tool — dark-first, streaks and goals up front, denser navigation. Both cover the five roadmap features you picked: word-by-word meanings, juz navigation, tajweed, multiple translations, streaks.
1a

Green demoted from a filled header bar to deep ink used sparingly, on a warm paper ground. Newsreader for English, Amiri for Arabic. No emoji. The reader loses all chrome; ayah numbers become inline circled numerals so the Arabic runs like a printed page. Word taps raise a meaning sheet.
